# Platform API specification

Version 3.2. This document is normative.

## 1. Overview

Purpose, audience, and the guarantees this service does and does not make.

#### 1.1 Detail 1

Requests are served from the regional edge closest to the caller. Clients
SHOULD NOT assume a single origin; responses carry `X-Region` for tracing.
Field ordering in responses is not stable and MUST NOT be relied upon.
Unknown fields are ignored on write and may appear on read after an upgrade.
Compatibility is evaluated per minor version. A field removed in 3.x is
announced under section 9 and continues to be served, empty, until sunset.
Callers MUST tolerate an empty value in any optional field at any time.
Retries MUST carry the original idempotency key; see section 6 for the rules.

## 4. Rate limiting

### 4.1 Per-endpoint limits

Every endpoint is limited independently. The default ceiling is **600 requests
per minute** per token. Write endpoints (`POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`) share a
single pool; read endpoints are counted separately.

### 4.2 Headers

Every response carries:

    X-RateLimit-Limit       600
    X-RateLimit-Remaining   417
    X-RateLimit-Reset       1738291200

`Reset` is a Unix timestamp, not a duration. Clients that treat it as seconds
remaining will back off for roughly fifty-five years.

### 4.3 Burst allowance

A token may exceed the per-minute ceiling by up to 20% for a single 10-second
window, provided the trailing 60-second average stays under the ceiling. The
burst allowance is not advertised in the headers.

### 4.4 Behavior at the limit

Exceeding the ceiling returns `429` with `Retry-After` in seconds. The service
does not queue. A `429` is never charged against the quota described in
section 7.

## 7. Quotas

### 7.1 Monthly and hourly quotas

Quotas are enforced in addition to the rate limits in section 4, and are
tracked per organization rather than per token.

    Plan        Hourly      Monthly
    Free        2,000       500,000
    Team        18,000      8,000,000
    Enterprise  custom      custom

### 7.2 Interaction with rate limiting

The two mechanisms are independent: a caller can be under the section 4 rate
limit and still exhaust the hourly quota. Note that the Team hourly quota of
18,000 is well below the 36,000 requests a single token could issue in an hour
at the section 4 ceiling of 600/minute, so on this plan the quota binds first
whenever sustained traffic runs above 300 requests per minute.

### 7.3 Exhaustion

Quota exhaustion returns `402`, not `429`. It is not retryable within the
window and `Retry-After` is not sent.
